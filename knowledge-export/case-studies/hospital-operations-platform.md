# Connecting patient intake, appointments, billing and hospital workflows in one operational platform.

A full-stack healthcare application bringing core patient and administrative workflows into a structured digital environment spanning registration, appointments, staff interfaces, billing and prescription management.

## Context

An application shaped around the hospital's operational journey.

- **Business environment:** Hospital and healthcare operations
- **Operational scope:** Patient intake, appointments, staff workflows, billing and prescriptions
- **Application model:** Full-stack web application supported by REST APIs
- **Engineering scope:** Frontend, backend, data and operational workflows

## Hospital workflows need consistency across every operational handoff.

Patient registration, appointment scheduling, staff activity, billing and prescription workflows each create information that needs to remain connected throughout the operational journey. The project required these activities to operate through a structured application rather than being treated as isolated software functions.

### Patient intake

Capture registration and patient information through a structured workflow.

### Appointment coordination

Support scheduling and appointment-related activity in one application environment.

### Staff visibility

Provide doctors and operational staff with role-relevant application views.

### Administrative continuity

Keep billing and prescription-related workflows connected to the wider platform.

## One application environment for connected hospital operations.

The solution combined application interfaces, REST-based services and structured data handling to support multiple hospital workflows through one operational platform.
