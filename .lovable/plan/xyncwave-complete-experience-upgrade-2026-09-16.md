# Xyncwave Complete Experience Upgrade

## Goal
Transform the existing site into a spacious, premium digital-transformation consulting experience that makes visitors recognize their problem, see relevant proof, and feel comfortable starting a useful conversation—without changing the approved brand or inventing evidence.

## What will change

### 1. Visual foundation and logo correction
- Fix the header and footer lockups so the complete approved artwork remains visible at every viewport and while scrolling.
- Preserve Carbon, White, Electric Blue, Slate, Graphite, Mist, and Inter exactly.
- Increase scale and intentional whitespace through responsive section-spacing and typography rules rather than arbitrary fixed heights.
- Add a restrained visual library for connected systems, data paths, architecture, automation, modernization, cloud, and AI.
- Alternate editorial white, Mist, Carbon, and selected visual scenes to create an art-directed rhythm.
- Add refined image/line reveals and arrow movement while honoring reduced-motion settings.

### 2. Shared content and experience architecture
- Extend the structured content models so solutions, industries, case studies, and insights explicitly reference each other and their contextual next action.
- Create reusable premium building blocks for digital scenes, transformation narratives, editorial stories, proof sections, and “Continue Exploring” journeys.
- Replace repeated small-card grids with asymmetric editorial layouts, large visual stories, horizontal rows, and full-width strategic moments.
- Keep evidence boundaries in the content model so editable metrics, testimonials, and unconfirmed details cannot appear as verified claims.

### 3. Homepage transformation
- Rebuild the opening around “What technology challenge is holding your business back?” with a large fragmentation-to-connection visual.
- Upgrade the problem-recognition section with four substantial editorial paths and contextual actions.
- Create the signature “What are you trying to solve?” selector with seven large choices and relevant destinations.
- Add a sophisticated complexity-to-clarity transformation narrative.
- Turn Track & Trace into a large evidence-led editorial story using verified supplied material.
- Upgrade insight discovery and the final emotional CTA while retaining one dominant action per decision area.

### 4. Complete commercial-page upgrade
- Deepen all solution and industry pages with larger editorial storytelling, contextual proof, relevant insights, and natural internal links.
- Show Track & Trace proof on Digital Transformation, Integration, ERP/Business Systems, and Logistics pages where relevant.
- Show the engineering-capacity delivery model on Engineering Capacity and Technology & IT Services pages without inventing client details.
- Upgrade listing pages from uniform grids to editorial capability and industry experiences.

### 5. Case-study experience
- Rebuild the listing as large alternating transformation stories.
- Rebuild Track & Trace around verified source material: technician/support workflow, ERPNext/Frappe web application, Flutter mobile application, AWS serverless application layer, job statuses, stock allocation and transfers, task chat, alerts, completion evidence, dashboards, role-based access, APIs, and operational visibility.
- Clearly distinguish delivered/verified material from forward-looking or scope-document expectations.
- Do not publish the documents’ unsupported savings or broad benefit claims as measured outcomes.
- Rebuild Engineering Capacity with substantial editable sections and explicit evidence placeholders until approved client-specific facts are available.
- Add technology, journey, results, related content, related solutions/industries, and challenge CTA sections.

### 6. Insights publication
- Redesign the insights home as an editorial publication with a visual lead story, varied story weights, and coherent custom artwork.
- Give each article a distinct high-quality transformation visual, stronger reading structure, executive summary, key takeaway, framework, relevant FAQ where useful, contextual CTA, and connected case study/solution/industry journey.
- Add natural Track & Trace references to the relevant digitalization article and reciprocal article links from the case study.

### 7. Conversation and conversion journeys
- Expand the opening selector to Operations, Engineering, AI, Modernization, Integration, Technology Strategy, and Something Else.
- Keep progressive details relevant to the selected challenge and retain the value-first, no-budget-required approach.
- Update navigation, mobile, page, article, case-study, and final CTAs to contextual curiosity-led language with selective trust microcopy.
- Refine confirmation pages around “Your challenge is with the team” and show the most relevant next content.

### 8. Quality assurance
- Check every content page for unique title, description, Open Graph title/description, heading hierarchy, accessibility, and meaningful alt text.
- Test all internal relationships and conversion paths with no dead ends or broken routes.
- Search the complete project for weak generic CTA labels and replace them only where a stronger contextual action applies.
- Visually test homepage, listings, representative solution/industry pages, both case studies, insights, an article, and the form at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Specifically verify full logos in desktop, mobile, sticky-header, menu-open, and footer states; check overflow, spacing, image loading, keyboard interactions, and form submission.
- Resolve all build, runtime, console, and network errors before completion.

## Visual assets
- Generate a cohesive family of original abstract digital-system visuals using the approved palette; no stock people, robots, cyberpunk, crypto, neon spectacle, or logo recreation.
- Use optimized responsive assets and lazy loading below the first viewport.
- Use supplied Track & Trace screenshots only when they are legible, non-sensitive, and materially improve the story; otherwise use architecture/workflow visuals based on verified facts.

## Evidence rules
- No fabricated clients, quotes, metrics, awards, certifications, ROI, delivery duration, or technology.
- Use editable internal fields or clearly labeled placeholders for unapproved proof.
- Treat source documents as reference evidence, not permission to expose private customer data shown inside screenshots.
